## Module 1: Trading basics: what trading really is

_25 min · free preview on the site_

Start from zero. Learn what a market is, why prices move, and how trading differs from investing and from gambling, with honest expectations from day one.

**Learning outcomes**

- Explain in your own words what a market and a trade are
- Describe why prices go up and down
- Tell the difference between trading, investing and gambling
- Set realistic expectations for your first months of learning

**Lessons**

1. What is a market?
2. Buyers, sellers and why prices move
3. Trading vs investing vs gambling
4. What a beginner should (and should not) expect
5. Key words you will hear every day

**Checklist**

- [ ] I can explain what a market is
- [ ] I know why prices move
- [ ] I understand that losses are a normal part of trading
- [ ] I have joined the Telegram community for Module 2
