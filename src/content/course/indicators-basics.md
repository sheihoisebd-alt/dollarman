## What this module covers

- **What indicators are.** Calculations based on past price and volume, which is why they lag.
- **Moving averages.** Simple and exponential moving averages for reading trend direction.
- **RSI and momentum.** What "overbought" and "oversold" really mean, and why they are not buy or sell buttons.
- **Less is more.** How to keep your chart to one or two indicators that support your plan.

## Practice task

Add a 50-period moving average to a daily chart. Describe in one sentence what it tells you about the trend.
