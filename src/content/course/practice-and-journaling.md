## What this module covers

- **Structured demo practice.** A practice period with your trading plan and fixed risk.
- **Keeping a trading journal.** What to log for every trade, using the [free journal template](/downloads).
- **Honest review.** Looking at your results and finding one thing to improve.
- **Common beginner mistakes**, and how to avoid them.
- **Next steps.** How to keep learning, and why there is no rush to trade live.

## Practice task

Log at least 20 demo trades in the journal, then do a weekly review and share one lesson you learned in the community.
