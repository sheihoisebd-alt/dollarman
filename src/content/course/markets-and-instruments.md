## What this module covers

- **Forex, stocks, crypto and commodities at a glance.** What is traded in each market, who the participants are, and how they differ in volatility and opening hours.
- **Currency pairs.** Why currencies are always quoted in pairs, how to read the base and quote currency in EUR/USD, and what "majors", "minors" and "crosses" mean.
- **Trading sessions.** The Asian, London and New York sessions, the busy overlap periods, and what those times are in Bangladesh (BST, UTC+6).
- **Liquidity and volatility.** Why some markets move smoothly and others jump, and why that matters for beginners.

## Practice task

Open a free chart and find EUR/USD, USD/JPY and GBP/USD. Write down the base and quote currency for each, and note what time each trading session opens in your local time.
