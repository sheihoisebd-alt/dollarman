## What this module covers

- **Why a written plan matters.** It turns emotional decisions into rules you can follow and review.
- **Your scope.** Which markets, timeframes and hours you will trade.
- **Entry and exit rules.** What must be true before you enter, where the stop-loss and target go, and when you do not trade.
- **The pre-trade checklist.** A short list you tick before every trade.
- **Weekly review.** A simple routine for looking back at your trades.

## Practice task

Write a one-page trading plan using the template shared in the Telegram community, and post it for feedback.
