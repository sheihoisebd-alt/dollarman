## What this module covers

- **What a broker does** and how brokers earn money (spread, commission, swap).
- **Leverage and margin** explained with simple numbers, and why leverage magnifies losses as much as gains.
- **Order types.** Market, limit and stop orders, and attaching a stop-loss and take-profit.
- **Regulation and scams.** How to check a broker's licence, and red flags such as guaranteed returns, "account managers" who trade for you, and pressure to deposit.
- **Legal checks.** Rules for online trading differ by country. In some countries, including Bangladesh, sending money abroad to trade forex or CFDs with overseas brokers may be restricted. Check your local laws before opening any live account.
- **Opening a demo account** to practise safely.

## Practice task

Open a demo account and place one market order and one limit order, both with a stop-loss. Close them and look at the trade history.
