## What this module covers

- **Support and resistance.** Price areas where buying or selling has repeatedly appeared, drawn as zones rather than exact lines.
- **Higher timeframes first.** Why the daily and 4-hour charts give you the clearest zones.
- **Trends and market structure.** Higher highs and higher lows, lower highs and lower lows, and breaks of structure.
- **Ranges and breakouts.** How to recognise a sideways market and why many breakouts fail.
- **Keeping it clean.** Why drawing too many lines leads to confusion.

## Practice task

Mark support and resistance zones on three different charts, label each as trending or ranging, and post one in the community for feedback.
