## What this module covers

- **Risk first.** Why protecting your account matters more than finding the perfect entry.
- **The 1% rule.** Risking a small, fixed percentage per trade, and how drawdowns add up.
- **Stop-loss placement.** Putting your stop where the trade idea is proven wrong.
- **Position sizing.** Turning your risk amount and stop distance into a lot size.
- **Risk/reward.** Comparing what you risk to what you aim to gain, and the break-even win rate.

Practise with the free [position-size](/en/tools/position-size-calculator), [risk/reward](/en/tools/risk-reward-calculator) and [pip value](/en/tools/pip-value-calculator) calculators.

## Practice task

Use the position-size calculator on three example trades with a 1% risk. Write down the lot size for each.
