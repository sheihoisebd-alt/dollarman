## What this module covers

- **Why emotions are the hardest part.** Knowing the rules is easy; following them under pressure is not.
- **FOMO and overtrading.** Recognising the urge to jump in, and how a checklist helps.
- **Revenge trading.** Why trying to "win it back" after a loss is so damaging.
- **Losing streaks.** Every trader has them. How to stay calm and reduce size.
- **Healthy routines.** Sleep, breaks and a clear stop-for-the-day rule.

## Practice task

Write your personal "stop trading for today" rule (for example, after two losses in a row) and keep it next to your screen.
