## 1. What is a market?

A **market** is simply a place where buyers and sellers agree on a price. A vegetable bazaar is a market. So is the global currency market, where banks, companies and individual traders exchange one currency for another every second of the working week.

When you **trade**, you buy or sell something hoping to benefit from a change in its price. You might buy a currency pair because you think its price will rise, or sell it because you think it will fall.

## 2. Buyers, sellers and why prices move

Every price you see on a chart is the last price at which a buyer and a seller agreed.

- When **more people want to buy** than sell at the current price, buyers must offer more, and the price **rises**.
- When **more people want to sell** than buy, sellers must accept less, and the price **falls**.

What makes people want to buy or sell? News, interest rates, company results, economic data, and also emotions such as fear and excitement. You do not need to predict every move. Your job as a trader is to find situations where the odds are reasonable and the risk is controlled.

## 3. Trading vs investing vs gambling

|              | Investing       | Trading                    | Gambling     |
| ------------ | --------------- | -------------------------- | ------------ |
| Time frame   | Months to years | Minutes to weeks           | One bet      |
| Based on     | Long-term value | Price behaviour and a plan | Luck         |
| Risk control | Diversification | Stop-loss, position size   | Usually none |

Trading **becomes gambling** when you trade without a plan, without a stop-loss, or with money you cannot afford to lose. Everything in this course is designed to help you avoid that.

## 4. What a beginner should (and should not) expect

Let's be honest from day one:

- **Most beginners lose money** when they start trading live too early. That is why we start on a demo account.
- **Losing trades are normal.** Even experienced traders are wrong often. What matters is how much you lose when you are wrong.
- **There are no shortcuts.** Anyone promising guaranteed profits or "risk-free" trading is not telling the truth.
- **Skills take time.** Plan to study and practise for several months before even thinking about real money.

> Our goal is to help you build real skills step by step, not to promise results. Treat trading as something you study, like a language or a craft.

## 5. Key words you will hear every day

- **Long / buy**: you expect the price to go up.
- **Short / sell**: you expect the price to go down.
- **Pip**: a small unit of price movement in forex (usually the 4th decimal place).
- **Spread**: the small difference between the buy and sell price; a cost of trading.
- **Stop-loss**: an order that closes your trade at a set loss, to protect your account.
- **Take-profit**: an order that closes your trade at a set profit.
- **Demo account**: a practice account with virtual money.
- **Leverage**: borrowed exposure from a broker. It magnifies losses as well as gains (Module 4).
