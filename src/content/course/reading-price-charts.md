## What this module covers

- **Chart types.** Line, bar and candlestick charts, and why most traders use candlesticks.
- **Anatomy of a candle.** Open, high, low and close (OHLC), bodies and wicks, bullish and bearish candles.
- **Timeframes.** From 5-minute to daily charts, how to combine a higher and a lower timeframe, and which ones suit beginners.
- **Common candle shapes.** Long wicks, small bodies and engulfing candles, plus why context matters more than the shape.
- **Setting up your first chart.** A clean layout on a free charting platform.

## Practice task

Set up a chart with a daily and a 1-hour timeframe. Pick five candles and write down their open, high, low and close.
