The most common reason beginners lose their whole account is not a bad strategy. It is **risking too much on each trade**. The 1% rule is a simple habit that helps prevent this.

## What is the 1% rule?

You decide never to risk more than **1% of your account** on a single trade. On a $500 account, that is $5 per trade.

It sounds small, and that is the point. A run of losing trades, which every trader experiences, will not wipe you out.

## Why it works

| Losing trades in a row | Account left at 1% risk | Account left at 10% risk |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
| 5                      | about 95%               | about 59%                |
| 10                     | about 90%               | about 35%                |

Small risk keeps you in the game long enough to learn.

## How to apply it in three steps

1. **Find your risk amount.** Account balance × 1%.
2. **Place your stop-loss** where your trade idea is proven wrong on the chart.
3. **Size your position** so that hitting the stop costs only your risk amount.

## A note on expectations

The 1% rule does not make you profitable by itself. It controls how much you lose when you are wrong, which gives you time to practise and improve. Always start on a demo account.
