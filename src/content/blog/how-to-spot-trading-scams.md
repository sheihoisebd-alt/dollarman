Trading attracts scammers because many beginners are hoping for quick results. Learning to spot the warning signs will protect your money.

## 7 red flags

1. **Guaranteed profits or "risk-free" trading.** Every real trade carries risk. Nobody can guarantee returns.
2. **"Send me money and I'll trade for you."** So-called account managers often disappear with deposits.
3. **Requests for your login or password.** A genuine educator or broker will never ask for them.
4. **Pressure to deposit quickly.** Countdown timers, "last chance" offers and constant messages are pressure tactics.
5. **Screenshots of huge profits.** They are easy to fake and prove nothing.
6. **Unregulated or unknown brokers.** Always check a broker's licence with the regulator's official register.
7. **Withdrawal problems.** If you are asked to pay a "fee" or "tax" before withdrawing, stop.

## How to stay safe

- Learn first and practise on a demo account.
- Check the laws in your country before trading with real money. Rules for online forex and CFD trading differ between countries.
- Never share passwords or one-time codes.
